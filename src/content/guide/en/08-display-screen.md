Plug a projector or a TV into the control desk computer over HDMI. In the top bar, the display screen button opens a second window and lets you choose the monitor; make it full screen with its own button or a double-click.

The display screen shows, in very large type:

- **the called match**, with its court and the players' bib numbers;
- **the previous calls** and the grid of courts playing, free or finished;
- **pool standings and brackets**, rotating automatically.

With two categories, matches are mixed and each one carries a highly visible category code. During a timed round, the screen shows the round's courts and a large shared countdown.

Nobody touches it: it updates by itself on every action of the control desk.
