Plug a projector or a TV into the control desk computer over HDMI. In the top bar, the display screen button opens a second window and lets you choose the monitor; make it full screen with its own button or a double-click.

The display screen shows, in turn, **the stages you chose**:

- **a pools stage**: every pool, with its standings (played, wins, set and point differences), the clubs and the matches on court;
- **a bracket**: the whole tree, folded in two around the final, each match's winner on top with a trophy and, in the matches for a place, the place won ("1st", "3rd"…).

A stage stays on screen for the time set in the settings (30 seconds by default); taller than the screen, it goes page by page. The time shows in a corner, and during a timed round, the shared countdown.

**Choosing the stages shown.** On the **Stages** screen, each stage has a **Display screen** box. It ticks itself at the stage's first match; tick it beforehand or untick it whenever you want (finished pools, for instance, to keep only the bracket).

Nobody touches it: it updates by itself on every action of the control desk.
