step: 5
title: Run the draw with seeds
summary: Pools drawn from present entrants, seeds placed in a snake, club mates kept apart.
---

In **Draw**, each category has its own section. Only present entrants (all their players checked in) are drawn.

1. **Rank your seeds**: add them with **Seed**, then put them in order by drag and drop or with the arrows. OpenShuttle has no federation ranking: you decide who the favourites are.
2. Click **Draw the pools**. Seeds are placed in a snake (A, B, C, C, B, A…), the others at random, each into the pool where their club has the fewest players.
3. **Check**: badges flag two players from the same club in a pool, or a pool of a different size. These are warnings, not blocks. Adjust by hand with drag and drop, or draw again.
4. Click **Confirm the draw**. The pool matches are then generated.

> A latecomer checked in after the draw shows up apart: drag them into a pool.
