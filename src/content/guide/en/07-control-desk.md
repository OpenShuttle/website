The **Control desk** is where you will spend most of the day. Each court has its card, in one of three states:

- **Free**: the next match proposed by the schedule, with the **Call** button. The match shows at once on the display screen;
- **Playing**: the entrants, the pool and the time played, with **End** when the match is over;
- **Finished**: the match over and the next one proposed, with **Enter score** and **Start next**.

**Pending scores.** No time to enter a score? Start the next match: the score waits in the **Pending scores** column, with its court and end time, and can be entered later in any order. Players' rest counts from the real end of the match.

**Entering a score.** The panel opens from the court, with as many sets as the win condition needs. Enter saves, Escape closes.

**Next matches.** The right column lists the queue, with a category filter, and says why a match is not ready: player resting for X minutes, player on another court. A proposed match with a player who has not had their rest is flagged; you can call it anyway.

**A forfeit.** In the score panel, **Forfeit** asks for confirmation. In a pool, every match of the entrant becomes a walkover: the win counts for the opponent, left out of sets and points, and the entrant ranks last in the pool. In a bracket, a forfeit counts as a loss.

**Timed matches.** A banner above the courts starts the round, shows the shared countdown, then **Whistle the end** sends every match to the pending scores. Nothing stops on its own: the control desk decides.

> A slip? **Undo**, in the top bar, reverts the last action.
