The **Schedule** forecasts every match left, court by court, with its estimated time, from the time on court of each win condition. It also sets the order of the calls.

**Before validation**, you work on **rounds**: round 1 of pool A, round 1 of pool B, the bracket rounds… The automatic order keeps everyone playing at about the same pace. Drag and drop the rounds in the list to change the order: the timeline beside it shows the result live. The rounds of a pool always keep their order.

With two categories, they share the courts **in turn** (round 1 of one category, round 1 of the other, then round 2…); you choose which one opens.

The **Court rules** card steers each court: **priority** (it takes the matches of what it concerns first), **only** (nothing else while matches it concerns are left) or **never**. A rule targets a category, a stage, a pool or a bracket round, on one or several courts at once. The timeline updates live.

On the **Info** screen, set the date and the start and end hours: the schedule starts from the start hour and flags a finish that runs too late. Add the **breaks** (lunch…) on the schedule: no match is planned across one. One badge per pool and bracket highlights its matches.

**Validate the schedule** once you are happy with the order: the match order is then fixed. You can then **move a single match** on the timeline by drag and drop. **Recompute** starts again from the automatic order, and **Back to blocks** reopens the order of the rounds.

The **Teams** tab shows how many matches each team has left. When a player has not had their minimum rest, the match is flagged: the control desk decides.
