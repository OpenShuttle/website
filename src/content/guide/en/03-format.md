title: Choose the format
summary: Pools, brackets, consolation and full placement, from a preset or tailor-made.
---

In **Format**, each category has its own format: a chain of **stages**. The easiest way is to click **Start from a preset…** and pick a ready-made format, for example:

- **Pools only**;
- **Pools + main bracket**;
- **Pools + main + consolation**;
- **Full placement bracket**, single elimination right from the draw.

You can also build the format yourself:

- **a pools stage**: pool sizes in order of preference (for example 4, else 5) or a fixed number of pools, tiebreak order, comparison across pools;
- **a bracket**: single elimination, with **every place played** (full placement) or ties by round lost;
- **advancement rules**: which ranks of which stage go to which later stage (ranks 1 to 3, the best 4th places, the rest…);
- **the win condition** of each stage: sets to win, points per set, point cap, or timed match with a golden point, and the estimated time on court used by the schedule.

Below the editor, the simulation shows for your number of entrants the pools, the number of matches and rounds, and the places played. A format you like can be saved as a preset, ready for your next tournaments.

> Timed matches are played in rounds: every court starts and ends together. They are only available when a single category plays.

The format can be changed until the first match of the category is called or scored.
