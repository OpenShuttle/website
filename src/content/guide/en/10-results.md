When the last match is over, the **Results** screen gives the **full standings** of each category, from first to last place, with each entrant's path. For every place to be unique, use full placement brackets; otherwise, entrants losing in the same round are tied.

- **Export the standings** saves a CSV file, to open in a spreadsheet or send to the clubs.
- **Close and archive** saves a copy of the tournament file where you choose, then closes it.

The archived file holds the whole tournament: entrants, draw, scores and history. Keep it for next year, and duplicate it to start again from the same format.

Enjoy your tournament!
