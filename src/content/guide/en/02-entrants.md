title: Add the entrants
summary: By hand or from a CSV file, with each player's club.

In **Entrants**, add the entrants of each category: a player in singles, a pair in doubles. Each player has a name (first and last name) and a club, picked from the club list or added on the way.

Already have the list in a spreadsheet? Use **Import…** with a CSV file with these columns:

| Category | Player 1 | Club 1 | Player 2 | Club 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MD | Lucas Perrin | BC Lyon | Félix Simon | BC Lyon |

The separator can be a semicolon or a comma, and the category is written with its short code. Invalid or already entered rows are skipped and reported by line number. The whole import is cancelled by a single **Undo**.

**Export** writes the same format: handy to keep the list from one year to the next.

> Two players with the same name? OpenShuttle warns you without blocking: namesakes exist. On the day, the bib number tells them apart.
