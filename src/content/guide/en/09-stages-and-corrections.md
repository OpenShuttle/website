title: Follow the stages and make corrections
summary: Pool standings, brackets, later stages and correction mode with history.
---

The **Stages** screen shows, category by category, the standings of each pool and the tree of each bracket.

**Pool tiebreaks** follow the order chosen in the format: wins, head-to-head (between exactly two entrants), set difference, point difference, then a random draw. Always differences, never ratios: walkovers stay neutral. If two entrants are still tied in a finished pool, **Draw lots** settles it.

**Later stages** are filled once the stages feeding them are complete: **Fill from the pools** places the qualified entrants by the advancement rules, keeping entrants from the same pool apart as long as possible. Check and swap two slots if needed before the first match.

**Correction mode** lets you, at any time:

- edit or clear a score;
- declare or cancel a forfeit;
- swap two matches not played yet.

Every action is recorded in the history, with its time, and can be undone. OpenShuttle warns about inconsistencies but never blocks an action that is possible.
