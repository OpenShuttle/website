---
step: 4
title: Check in the players
summary: One click per player, one bib per entrant, late entries added on site.
---

On the day, open **Check-in**. The list shows each player with their category, partner and bib, and a search by name, club or bib number.

- Click **Present** when a player arrives. A pair is complete when both players are checked in; **Check in the pair** checks in both at once.
- The **bib number** is given at the pair's first check-in. It then shows everywhere next to the name, on the control desk as on the display screen.
- Counters at the top show where you stand, for example “40 / 42 players · 20 / 21 complete pairs”.
- A last-minute entry? **Add on site**. Someone absent? Simply remove them from the list.
